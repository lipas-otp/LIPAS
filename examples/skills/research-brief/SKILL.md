---
name: research-brief
description: Turn a small set of sources into an evidence-aware research brief.
---
When preparing a research brief:

1. Search the available sources before drawing conclusions.
2. Separate source facts from your own synthesis.
3. State uncertainty, missing evidence, and important limitations.
4. Do not invent papers, citations, methods, or results.
5. End with a concise recommendation for the next research step.
