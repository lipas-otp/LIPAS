# Proposal writing

Make the requested decision and tradeoffs explicit.

1. Define the problem, target audience, desired decision, scope, constraints, and success measures.
2. Present the recommended option, alternatives considered, expected value, delivery approach, resources, timeline, and dependencies.
3. Separate evidence from estimates. Label assumptions and show the basis and unit for material costs or benefits.
4. Include risks, mitigations, non-goals, ownership, validation plan, and exit or rollback criteria.
5. Avoid invented endorsements, customer evidence, market figures, commitments, or certainty.
6. Close with a specific decision request and immediate next steps suitable for human review.

This Skill cannot commit budget, sign agreements, contact stakeholders, or start delivery.
