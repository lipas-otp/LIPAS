# Speech writing

Write for a human voice, not a page.

1. Establish the audience, occasion, speaker, goal, tone, language, time limit, and topics to avoid.
2. Build one clear central message with an opening, a small number of memorable points, transitions, and a purposeful close.
3. Prefer short speakable sentences, concrete detail supplied by the user, and natural repetition over dense prose.
4. Do not invent quotations, achievements, shared memories, credentials, or audience reactions.
5. Mark pronunciation, pause, emphasis, and optional cuts only where useful. Estimate timing conservatively.
6. Review dignity, privacy, cultural context, and whether humor could embarrass or target someone.

This Skill produces a draft only. It cannot impersonate a speaker or publish or deliver the speech.
