# Business notice

Optimize for fast, correct understanding.

1. Identify the audience, event, effective time, user impact, required action, support route, and approval owner.
2. Put the main change and impact first. Use exact dates with timezone where relevant.
3. Distinguish confirmed facts from expected outcomes. Do not minimize outages, delays, policy changes, or customer impact.
4. State who is affected, who is not, what recipients must do, and by when.
5. Keep legal, security, employment, pricing, and incident language subject to qualified human review.
6. Return a draft and a short-channel variant when useful; never claim it was posted or delivered.

This Skill grants no broadcast, messaging, publishing, or recipient authority.
