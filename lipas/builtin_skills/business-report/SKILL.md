# Business report

Make the report useful for a decision or follow-up action.

1. Establish the audience, reporting period, decision or question, expected length, and source material.
2. Lead with an executive summary, then separate facts, analysis, decisions, risks, and requested actions.
3. Preserve dates, owners, quantities, units, and definitions. Label estimates, assumptions, missing data, and conflicting sources.
4. Use tables only when they improve comparison. Avoid decorative metrics and unsupported causal claims.
5. End with named actions, owners, deadlines, dependencies, and unresolved questions when supplied.
6. Review confidentiality, internal attribution, and whether each important conclusion is supported by the provided evidence.

This Skill drafts text only. It cannot access company systems, certify figures, or distribute a report.
