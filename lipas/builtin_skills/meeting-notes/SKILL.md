# Meeting notes

Distinguish what happened from what is inferred.

1. Record the meeting purpose, date, participants, and source completeness only when provided.
2. Separate discussion context, decisions, action items, risks, parking-lot topics, and unresolved questions.
3. Preserve exact owners, dates, amounts, and commitments. Use `TBD` rather than inventing an owner or deadline.
4. Attribute sensitive or contested statements only when attribution is necessary and supported by the source.
5. Remove repetition without erasing dissent, conditions, dependencies, or reversals.
6. End with a review checklist for ambiguous decisions and actions before the notes are shared.

This Skill cannot record a meeting, inspect a calendar, notify participants, or create tasks without separate Tools.
