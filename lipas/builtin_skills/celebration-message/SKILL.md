# Celebration message

Match warmth to the relationship and occasion.

1. Establish the recipient, relationship, occasion, language, tone, length, and delivery context.
2. Use only memories, traits, names, and wishes supplied by the user. Prefer one genuine detail to generic exaggeration.
3. Avoid jokes about age, health, fertility, relationships, identity, work, or money unless the user clearly establishes they are welcome.
4. Do not imply intimacy, promises, group consensus, or private knowledge that was not provided.
5. Offer concise, warm, formal, playful, or poetic variants when useful.
6. Preserve privacy and return text for review without claiming it was sent or posted.

This Skill grants no contact, posting, gift, or delivery capability.
